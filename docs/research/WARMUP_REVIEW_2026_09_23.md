# Warm-up evidence review and approved v2 rules

Prepared September 23, 2026. Status: APPROVED by the owner in conversation: “yes I approve this”. Rule version: `owner-warmup-v2`. The v1 calculation is retained for historical compatibility; v2 adds the approved bodyweight/assistance targets and continuation policy. This document is research synthesis and a concrete policy proposal, not independent clinical review or catalog activation.

## Question and search scope

What preparation should the private app prescribe before the owner's external-load exercises, mixed unassisted/machine-assisted pull-ups, supported captain's-chair knee raises and kneeling ab-wheel rollouts?

Targeted web searches located peer-reviewed reviews, original trials and official guidance through the review date. Searches covered resistance-training warm-up/re-warm-up, upper-body preparation, static stretching, pull-ups, captain's-chair knee raises and ab-wheel exercise. This is a targeted evidence review, not an exhaustive systematic review. Abstracts and publisher-indexed text were used where full text could not be accessed; access limits are identified below. Search dates displayed by search engines were not treated as publication dates. No social-media or commercial training advice establishes a scientific rule here.

## Findings and limitations

| Source | Relevant finding | Limits and implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Neves et al., 2026, scoping review](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42978-025-00361-9), DOI 10.1007/s42978-025-00361-9 | Nineteen warm-up studies and one re-warm-up study in strength-trained males; benefits were reported for selected maximal-load, velocity, power and repetition outcomes. Search updated November 2025. | Publisher abstract reviewed. Heterogeneous protocols and acute outcomes; no universal optimum, no validation of this owner's full routine or long-term growth. |
| [Ribeiro et al., 2021, systematic review](https://revistas.rcaap.pt/motricidade/article/view/21143), DOI 10.6063/motricidade.21143 | Eleven included articles; general-plus-specific preparation and specific warm-ups had mixed performance results. | Publisher abstract reviewed. Supports uncertainty rather than mandatory elaborate preparation for every exercise. |
| [Viveiros et al., 2024, original trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39593476/), DOI 10.1016/j.jbmt.2024.08.004 | Fifteen trained men compared 15 reps at 40%, 10 at 60%, and 5 at 80% of 10RM before bench press, leg press and pulldown. The 80%/5-rep condition produced greater total workout volume than the other conditions. | Abstract reviewed. Both load and reps changed, so the result cannot isolate load as the cause. Working sets went to failure, unlike our target 2–3 RIR. Does not establish 80% as universally best. |
| [Enes et al., 2025, original trial](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666337625000988), DOI 10.1016/j.smhs.2025.08.002 | One set of 3–4 reps at 75% 10RM, two sets at 55%/75% 10RM, and no specific warm-up produced similar performance/perceptual responses for bench press and 45-degree leg press. | Published abstract retrieved through indexed publisher text; full-text fetch unavailable. The earlier preprint was not treated as an additional trial. Findings are exercise/protocol-specific, not proof that preparation never matters. |
| [Ribeiro et al., 2014, original trial](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25153744/), DOI 10.2466/25.29.PMS.119c17z7 | Fifteen men completed bench press, squat and curls at 80% 1RM under no/specific/aerobic/combined warm-up conditions; no significant difference in total reps or fatigue index was found. | Abstract reviewed. Small acute study; a nonsignificant difference does not establish universal equivalence or injury outcomes. |
| [Behm et al., 2016, systematic review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26642915/), DOI 10.1139/apnm-2015-0235 | Immediate performance reductions after static stretching were larger for at least 60 seconds per muscle group than shorter exposure; subsequent dynamic activity modified the interpretation. | Abstract reviewed. Do not describe all stretching as harmful or 60 seconds as a precise biological cutoff. Basic stretching alone is not a tested substitute for this proposed rehearsal routine. |
| [McCrary et al., 2015, systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2014-094228), DOI 10.1136/bjsports-2014-094228 | Thirty-one studies of upper-body warm-up found benefits for certain high-load dynamic protocols; none investigated injury-prevention outcomes. | Publisher-indexed article text reviewed. Performance findings do not establish that our easy-rehearsal protocol prevents injuries. Do not generalize the historical absence to all later research or other sports. |
| [Neves et al., 2025, re-warm-up trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12115624/), DOI 10.3390/sports13050142 | Twenty-two men completed randomized bench/squat sequences. Re-warm-up improved several squat mechanical outcomes after bench press; bench-press benefits after squat were less evident. | Full text reviewed. Supports exercise-dependent effects, not a rule to repeat a full general warm-up before every movement. |
| [American Heart Association guidance](https://www.heart.org/en/healthy-living/exercise-and-physical-activity/fitness-basics/warm-up-cool-down), reviewed by AHA January 16, 2024 | Recommends gradually warming up for 5–10 minutes. | General guidance, not a trial validating precisely five minutes of walking for this program. Retaining five minutes is an explicit product choice within that guidance. |

The evidence supports considering movement-specific preparation, but confidence in a universal performance benefit is limited by varied populations, exercises and outcomes. Confidence in the exact proposed doses below is low: they are practical defaults, not a formally graded guideline. No direct comparative study establishing optimal warm-up reps/rests for supported knee raises or kneeling ab-wheel rollouts was located. Pull-up protocols found in research methods do not prove those protocols are optimal; their use in a study is not a warm-up comparison. No long-term hypertrophy or injury-prevention benefit is claimed for this proposal.

## Approved policy

The purpose is brief, repeatable preparation and an opportunity to report that a familiar movement feels unusually difficult, while limiting extra work. The exact rep counts, effort response and rest times below are product-policy choices informed by the evidence and current program. They must not be described as scientifically established optima.

### W1 — General preparation (retain)

Five minutes of easy walking once at the start of the session. No speed, incline, heart-rate threshold or calorie target is inferred. Record actual duration. A timer ending does not require the user to start lifting immediately, and additional time is allowed without a deadline penalty.

### W2 — Stretching (clarify)

Do not prescribe static stretching as the sole required warm-up. Personal stretching remains optional; prefer placing prolonged static holds outside the immediate pre-lifting preparation. Brief comfortable stretching is not prohibited. No new stretching sequence, automatic range-of-motion test or mandatory stretching timer is introduced.

### W3 — External-load exercises (retain existing approved defaults)

Before the first external-load exercise: 8 reps at no more than 50% of the confirmed working setting, rest 60 seconds, then 5 reps at no more than 75%, rest 90 seconds. Before each later external-load exercise: 5 reps at no more than 50%, rest 60 seconds. Rehearse both exercises of a superset separately before the first work round. Friday's incline press remains the first external-load exercise even though pull-ups precede it.

Round down using available verified settings and the same load convention. These are percentages of the recorded working setting, not 1RM or effective machine resistance. Do not guess unloaded-machine resistance. Missing feasible settings return setup-required. The review does not establish superiority of these exact percentages; retaining them avoids changing a previously approved policy without compelling evidence. Do not add heavier potentiation sets or failure attempts.

### W4 — Owner's pull-up sequence (new)

Before the first working pull-up set:

1. Perform **5 machine-assisted rehearsal reps** at a separately confirmed comfortably easy assistance setting on the identified machine. Rest **60 seconds**.
2. Perform **2 unassisted rehearsal reps** in the same verified grip/setup as the unassisted working sets. Rest **120 seconds** before starting working sets.

The second rehearsal applies only when the planned working sequence includes verified unassisted pull-ups. For an approved entirely assisted sequence, prescribe the first rehearsal only and rest **120 seconds** after it; do not introduce unassisted reps to an assisted-only user.

The owner currently reports two unassisted working sets and one assisted set, approximately 70 lb assistance. That approximate recollection does not supply an exact warm-up value. Store an independently confirmed rehearsal-assistance setting; no percentage of bodyweight, subtraction from bodyweight, or percentage of the assistance label determines it. The intended easy setting must be confirmed rather than assumed merely from a numeric ordering of settings. The same machine's verified assistance semantics must be preserved. No automatic drop in assistance after successful rehearsals.

If that easy setting or the unassisted setup is missing, return setup-required for the applicable branch. If a rehearsal is not easy/controlled, pause the affected exercise for review; do not automatically proceed or create an easier substitute. These rules do not approve the separate mixed-working-set history/progression contract.

### W5 — Supported captain's-chair knee raises (new)

Before the working sets, perform **5 controlled bent-knee rehearsal reps**, followed by **60 seconds** of rest. Use the exact confirmed supported station, with the back and forearms supported, and the same confirmed comfortable knee-raise range as the working variation. No added weight, straight-leg progression or hanging variation is inferred. The rehearsal must feel easy and controlled. Missing setup/range confirmation returns setup-required.

This proposal explicitly concerns the owner's supported variation. The original hanging-knee-raise template requires a new reviewed binding/version before app execution; historical records must retain their original identity.

### W6 — Kneeling ab-wheel rollout (new)

Before the working sets, perform **3 controlled kneeling rehearsal reps**, followed by **60 seconds** of rest. Use a separately recorded short, comfortable rehearsal endpoint no farther than the confirmed working endpoint. The endpoint is a repeatable setup reference confirmed by the user, not an invented percentage, angle or distance. No standing rollout or added load is prescribed. If a controlled easy rehearsal endpoint has not been established, return setup-required rather than guessing one.

A shortened rehearsal is explicitly logged as a rehearsal; it is never counted as a full-range working set. The working range and prescribed work remain unchanged.

### W7 — Execution and missing-data rules (apply to all rehearsals)

- Before advancing, request an explicit `easy_and_controlled` response. `not_easy_or_not_controlled` pauses that exercise for setup review; `unknown` or missing feedback also pauses. Reps alone cannot infer readiness. Numeric RIR is not required for rehearsal feedback; working-set RIR remains separate.
- Pain or concerning symptoms use the existing safety-stop path, preserve the report and prohibit automatic replacement or increased prescription. This review supplies no new diagnosis or symptom-clearance rule.
- Rest timers are minimum planned pauses, not commands to start. The user may take longer; completion also requires their explicit continuation action.
- Rehearsals are always non-working sets and excluded from progression streaks. They cannot confirm a new working baseline automatically.
- Each prescribed rehearsal occurs once before that movement's work, not before every working set. For a superset, finish both rehearsals before round one.
- Resuming a saved session must preserve completed rehearsals and prevent accidental duplicate records. A prolonged-interruption re-warm-up threshold is not established here; an interrupted flow must request preparation review instead of inventing a cutoff or automatically authorizing resumed working sets.
- Count actual walking, rehearsal execution, rest and transitions toward session duration. Duration preference stays advisory. No exact total is promised without execution/transition inputs.

## Required data and implementation examples

Inputs: approved rule version; exact profile/slot/variation/setup IDs; current safety and eligibility result; baseline/target revision; warm-up purpose; confirmed equipment settings or range-reference IDs; explicit sequence branch; completed rehearsal IDs; current feedback. Neither names nor free text select a branch.

Outputs: immutable ordered rehearsal targets with variant/setup, external load or assistance quantity (never conflated), range reference, reps/rest, `isWorkingSet=false`, evidence/rule references and ready/setup-required/paused/safety-stop result. Rehearsal readiness authorizes only the next explicitly planned action, not a new program or safety clearance.

| Case | Expected approved behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Verified mixed pull-up plan, separately verified easy assistance setting A and unassisted setup B | A × 5; 60-sec rest; B × 2; 120-sec rest |
| Only input is remembered assistance of about 70 lb | Setup-required; no exact assistance target invented |
| Approved assisted-only plan with verified easy setting | Assisted × 5; 120-sec rest; no unassisted rehearsal |
| Missing unassisted setup for mixed branch | Setup-required; no partial protocol represented as complete |
| Captain's-chair setup and range confirmed | 5 rehearsal reps; 60-sec rest; never labeled hanging |
| Kneeling rollout working and short rehearsal endpoints confirmed | 3 rehearsal reps at rehearsal endpoint; 60-sec rest |
| Rollout endpoint absent or farther than confirmed working endpoint | Setup-required; do not extend range automatically |
| Feedback easy/controlled and rest elapsed, explicit continue | Advance only to next planned action |
| Feedback unknown, missing, or not easy/controlled | Pause; no auto-advance, load change or substitute |
| Symptom report despite completed warm-up | Safety stop takes precedence |
| Rehearsal repetitions logged | Retained as rehearsal only; zero progression exposures created |
| Session resumed after interruption | Restore records without duplicates; request preparation review |
| Total exceeds selected duration | Advisory estimate; do not remove rests or working sets |
| Same explicit inputs and settings in different order | Identical targets and explanation codes |

Implementation requires normal/boundary/invalid/missing-data tests and separate application tests for stop/continue, duplicate actions, interrupted sessions, mixed quantity semantics, versioned variation bindings and exclusion from progression. Owner approval of this policy does not automatically approve catalog mappings or verify equipment.
