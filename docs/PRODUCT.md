# Product Specification

Status: draft template; complete and approve before workout-engine implementation.

## Problem

Intermediate lifters need workouts that adapt to actual performance, available equipment, schedule, and recovery without requiring them to design each session manually.

## Initial target user

To be confirmed. Proposed starting segment: intermediate recreational lifters training primarily for hypertrophy three to five days per week.

## Core promise

To be confirmed. Proposed promise: open the app and receive a clear, explainable workout that adapts to recent training while remaining quick to log in the gym.

## V1 candidate scope

- Local onboarding and training profile
- Equipment and exercise constraints
- Deterministic workout generation
- Set logging with reps, load, and RIR
- Rest timer
- Workout history
- Basic progression and volume insights
- Local export and deletion

## Explicitly outside early V1

- Social feed
- Coaching marketplace
- Live AI workout generation
- Wearable integrations
- Nutrition tracking
- Cloud synchronization

## Decisions required from the product owner

1. Exact target lifter and minimum training experience.
2. Primary goal supported at launch.
3. Supported schedules and session-duration limits.
4. Required equipment profiles.
5. Meaning of readiness and whether subjective inputs affect it.
6. How recommendations explain themselves.
7. Free versus paid feature boundary.
8. Success criteria for a four-week beta.

## Proposed beta success criteria

These are placeholders, not approved targets:

- 30 intermediate lifters begin a four-week beta.
- At least 60% finish three or more workouts per week.
- At least 50% complete the four-week program.
- At least 40% say they would pay at the intended price.
- Serious or unsafe recommendation reports are investigated before launch.
